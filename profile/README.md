## Hi, we're Meganet Systems.

Good technology should solve problems, not create more of them. We help individuals, communities and businesses with everything from troublesome computers to server infrastructure, reliable websites and custom software.

Our approach is simple: **understand the problem, build a practical solution and make sure it stays maintainable.** No unnecessary complexity. No jargon for the sake of it.

## What we do

| IT support & remote assistance | Servers & infrastructure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Troubleshooting, hardware and software support, device setup and practical fixes. | Linux servers, virtualisation, networking, migrations and ongoing system maintenance. |
| **Hosting & web** | **Monitoring & maintenance** |
| Website development, managed hosting, DNS, SSL, Cloudflare and deployment. | Availability monitoring, service health, backups and keeping systems dependable. |

> From the small fixes to the big builds, we look after the technology behind the experience.
